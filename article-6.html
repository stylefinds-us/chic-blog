The Pieces Worth Spending More On

August 2026 · 6 min read
Advertisement space

A practical framework for deciding where quality matters and where you can save. This guide focuses on practical ideas that can be adapted to your own wardrobe, budget and lifestyle.

Spend on the pieces you wear constantly

If you reach for an item several times a week, better construction and fabric can make a meaningful difference.

Consider shoes and bags carefully

These pieces take a lot of wear. Comfortable construction, strong hardware and materials that age well can be worth prioritising.

Save on trend pieces

If something is likely to feel dated quickly, a lower-cost option can let you experiment without committing too much of your wardrobe budget.

Fit beats a famous label

A well-fitting, simple garment can look more polished than an expensive piece that does not suit your proportions.

Build slowly

Instead of replacing everything at once, upgrade the pieces that cause the most frustration first.
