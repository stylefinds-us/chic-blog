7 Easy Outfit Formulas That Always Look Expensive

August 2026 · 6 min read
Advertisement space

Simple styling is often the secret behind a polished wardrobe. You do not need a closet full of statement pieces to make an outfit look considered.

1. A simple top + tailored trousers

Pair a clean T-shirt, fine knit or crisp shirt with trousers that have a defined shape. Add a structured bag and simple shoes for an instantly polished finish.

2. One colour, different textures

Monochrome looks become more interesting when the fabrics change. Try denim with cotton, knitwear with satin, or leather details with a soft woven piece.

3. The third-piece rule

Start with a basic top and bottom, then add one intentional layer: a blazer, cardigan, overshirt or lightweight jacket.

4. Denim + elevated accessories

Let your favourite jeans do the casual work and use accessories to raise the level: a belt, clean handbag, watch, jewellery or refined shoes.

5. Dress + practical layer

A simple dress becomes much more versatile with a jacket, cardigan or shirt worn over it.

6. Relaxed bottom + fitted top

Balance wide-leg trousers or relaxed jeans with a more defined top. The contrast creates shape without making the outfit complicated.

7. Basics + one focal point

Keep most of the outfit quiet and let one item lead — a striking bag, shoe, jacket or piece of jewellery.

The goal is not to follow every trend. Build a small collection of formulas that work for your lifestyle, then change the details when you want a fresh look.
