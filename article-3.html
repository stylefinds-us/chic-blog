How to Build a Chic Outfit Without Overthinking It

August 2026 · 5 min read
Advertisement space

When an outfit feels off, adding more pieces is rarely the answer. Start by checking proportion, colour and the final details.

Check the proportions

Balance is more important than following a specific silhouette. Try a fitted piece with something relaxed, or a shorter top with a higher-rise bottom.

Keep the colour story simple

Two or three related colours can look more intentional than a collection of unrelated shades. Neutrals are especially useful as a base.

Add one finishing detail

A belt, bag, jewellery, sunglasses or a great pair of shoes can make a basic outfit feel complete.

Stop before it becomes too much

If you already have a strong jacket and interesting shoes, you probably do not need three more statement accessories. Let one or two details do the work.

Good personal style is often the result of editing. Put the outfit on, remove one unnecessary element, and see if it becomes clearer.
