5 Small Details That Make Basics Look Better

August 2026 · 6 min read
Advertisement space

Use accessories, proportions and layers to give simple outfits a more elevated finish. This guide focuses on practical ideas that can be adapted to your own wardrobe, budget and lifestyle.

Pay attention to proportions

Balance loose pieces with something more defined. A relaxed trouser can work beautifully with a fitted knit or a tucked-in shirt.

Use a deliberate tuck

A full tuck, half tuck or clean front tuck can change the shape of an outfit without adding another item.

Repeat a colour

Echo one colour in two places, such as shoes and a bag or jewellery and a belt. The repetition makes the outfit feel considered.

Keep accessories intentional

You do not need many accessories. One strong bag, watch, pair of earrings or sunglasses can be enough.

Let fabric do some work

Mixing matte and subtle shine, soft and structured textures, or smooth and tactile fabrics adds depth to basics.
